📚 Study Overview

Tamil Nadu can be studied through its physical geography, history, administration, economy, culture and important places. Use this page as a starting point and add your own classroom notes or reference material.

🗺️ Geography

Physical setting: Southern and eastern coastal plains.

Topics to revise: rivers, climate, soils, agriculture, forests, minerals, major cities and neighbouring states.

📜 History

Suggested revision topics: ancient and medieval heritage, major dynasties, important movements, colonial-era developments, independence movement and post-independence milestones.

🎨 Art, Culture & Festivals

Revise local languages, literature, traditional arts, music, dance, clothing, cuisine, fairs and important festivals of Tamil Nadu.

🏞️ Important Places

Chennai, Madurai, Ooty.
